# DAHAB — PRODUCT OPERATING BLUEPRINT
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 2 -->

## 1. PRODUCT

### Platform

Web-first, mobile-first, installable/PWA-capable travel companion.

### Primary Market

Dahab, South Sinai, Egypt.

### Brand

Dahab is the destination and the product brand.

The product should not feel like:
- a generic travel marketplace
- a generic AI assistant
- a hotel booking clone
- a tourist guide
- a generic SaaS dashboard

It should feel like:
> "Dahab itself, turned into an intelligent digital companion."

---

# 2. PRODUCT MISSION

Dahab helps people discover, plan, experience, book, navigate, and remember Dahab through one continuously adapting system.

The platform should replace fragmented behavior such as:

Google
+ Instagram
+ TikTok
+ WhatsApp
+ Booking
+ Tripadvisor
+ maps
+ local recommendations
+ random saved posts
+ spreadsheets
+ notes

with one connected travel experience.

The platform should understand:

- where the traveler is
- where they are going
- what they care about
- what they have already done
- what they have booked
- what time it is
- where they are
- what is available now
- what is relevant next

---

# 3. CORE PRODUCT MECHANISM

The platform understands the traveler journey as:

6. Preparing
7. Arriving

- trip progress

- unfinished plans

and adapt the product accordingly.

- pricing

- inventory
- payments
- supplier status

- interaction
